End-to-end encrypted async voice interviews. Source-protective by design.

OffMic lets a journalist, researcher, or human rights worker collect a recorded answer from a source — without exposing the recording to a third party. Audio is encrypted in the source's browser before upload. The OffMic server stores ciphertext only. We cannot read what we hold.

01What OffMic is

End-to-end encrypted AES-256-GCM. The key lives in the URL fragment and is never sent to the server.
Browser-only for sources No account. No install. No Tor required. Works in any modern browser.
Open source, AGPL-3.0 Fully auditable. External cryptographic audit planned post-MVP.
Privacy-first by default No analytics, no IP logging, no third-party trackers. Hosted in the EU.

02What OffMic is not

  • Not a replacement for SecureDrop. If your threat model includes state-level adversaries with active network or device access, use SecureDrop instead.
  • Not a real-time call tool. For synchronous encrypted communication, use Signal.
  • Not a generic recorder. OffMic is structured around the journalist↔source interview flow, not freeform capture.
