End-to-end encrypted
 async voice interviews.
Source-protective by design.

OffMic lets a journalist, researcher, or human rights worker collect a recorded answer from a source without exposing the recording to a third party. Audio encrypted in the browser. Server sees ciphertext only. We cannot read what we hold.
